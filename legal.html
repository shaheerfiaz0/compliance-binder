Who we are

Compliance Binder ("we," "us") publishes informational guides and template documents for childcare providers navigating state licensing requirements. Purchases are processed through a third-party platform (currently Gumroad); we do not directly store your payment card details.

What we sell

Our products are digital, informational documents — parent handbooks, policy manuals, and forms — intended as a drafting starting point. They are not legal advice, and purchasing a bundle does not create any advisory or attorney-client relationship. Regulations referenced in our products are cited to their source wherever possible; where we could not verify a figure against a primary source, we say so directly in the document.

Your responsibility as a buyer

  • Verify all state-specific figures (ratios, fees, hours) against your state licensing agency before relying on them for an inspection or legal filing.
  • Have a licensed attorney or childcare compliance consultant in your state review any document before using it in your program.
  • Understand that regulations change after our last verification date, printed on the cover of each document.

Refund policy

Because our products are instant digital downloads, we don't offer refunds for buyer's remorse after download. This is why a free guide covering the same core content is available before you buy — read it first to judge quality and fit.

If you find a factual error tied to an outdated or misstated regulation, contact us. We will correct the error and send you the updated file at no cost. If the error is significant and we can't correct it promptly, we'll offer a refund at our discretion.

Updates to purchased products

If we become aware that a cited regulation has materially changed, buyers of the affected state bundle will be offered a free updated version. We don't guarantee proactive monitoring of every regulatory change in real time — if your compliance decision is time-sensitive, verify directly with your licensing agency rather than relying solely on our update cycle.
